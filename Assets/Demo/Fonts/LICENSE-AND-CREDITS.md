Fonts under `Assets/Demo/Fonts/` are **demo content only**. They are not part of the copyable package (`Assets/TmpFontPipeline/`). When you copy TmpFontPipeline into another project, supply your own fonts and licenses.

| Folder | Atlas mode | Role in this repo |
|--------|------------|-------------------|
| `EN/`, `KO/`, `JP/`, `SC/`, `TC/` (Static SDF) | Static | Localized UI / dialogue from `String*.json` |
| `Dynamic/` | Dynamic | Unpredictable multilingual text (e.g. leaderboard names) |
